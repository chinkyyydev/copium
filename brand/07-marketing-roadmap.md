# 07 — Marketing Roadmap & Growth Strategy

## 1. Brand Positioning

**Category:** AI virtual influencer / internet personality.
**Position:** *The internet's coping mechanism, personified.* Not "an AI experiment" (cold), not "a VTuber" (crowded), not "a meme account" (ceiling). She is a **character** — the difference between a Twitter gimmick and an IP.

**Positioning statement:** For terminally online people in tech, gaming, and crypto who process everything through humor, Copium is the AI companion who turns every L into a shared joke — unlike meme accounts (no personality), chatbots (no soul), and human influencers (asleep 8 hours a day).

**Differentiators to hammer in all marketing:** (1) she never sleeps and reacts first, (2) she remembers you, (3) she's a character with lore, not a content account, (4) the craft is premium while the content is unhinged.

## 2. Pre-Launch (Weeks −4 to 0)

- **Asset lock:** 30 evergreen posts banked, 15 expression artworks, model sheet, website v1 (chat + dose), Discord shell.
- **Mystery seeding (week −2):** the account posts cryptic in-character fragments before any announcement — "booting…", "calibrating optimism… 400% (above safe limits)", a glitched silhouette. Anime-style teaser culture; costs nothing, primes the reveal.
- **The Reveal (day 0):** single self-contained thread: who she is, 3 killer jokes, the character art, link to the clinic. The thread must work as a screenshot.
- Handle, domains, and trademark search cleared before any public post. Register "COPIUM" wordmark + glitch-pill mark in key classes (software, entertainment, apparel) early — mascot IP value lives in the mark.

## 3. Growth Ladder

### → 100 followers (Week 1) — "Prove the voice"
Tactics: reply-section land grab (doc 04 §5) as the primary engine — 30+ quality replies/day on viral tech/gaming posts; the profile converts visitors via a pinned intro thread. Founder's personal network seeds the first 50. **Metric that matters: profile-visit → follow rate >10%** (if lower, the bio/pinned content is broken — fix before scaling).

### → 1,000 (Weeks 2–6) — "Prove the format"
- Launch Daily Dose + Patch Notes series (rituals begin).
- First 10 "whale replies": landing a top reply on a 1M+ impression post converts hundreds at once. Speed on outages/releases is the whole edge.
- Personally welcome early followers in replies; first 1,000 are future evangelists and should feel like founding members ("Patient #0042" is a status symbol — issue numbered patient cards via the website).
- Begin weekly fan-art celebration even if the "fan art" is three people.

### → 10,000 (Months 2–5) — "Prove the community"
- **Discord opens** ("the waiting room"). Patient-card verification links site accounts.
- First viral swing content: Chart of the Week + a "she replied to me" reputation — screenshots of her personalized, memory-aware replies are the organic marketing.
- **Micro-collabs:** reply-banter with 5k–50k tech/gaming shitposters (peer tier, mutual benefit, no payment — banter is the collab).
- First earned-media push: pitch the "autonomous AI influencer with actual jokes" story to tech newsletters/YouTubers covering AI. The character IS the demo.

### → 100,000 (Months 5–12) — "Prove the brand"
- **Mid-tier collabs:** VTuber guest banter, podcast "interviews" with the AI (she does interviews in-character — reliably viral format), collabs with AI/dev-tool brands who want her to roast their launch (paid, brand-safe, in-voice or no deal).
- Merch drop #1 (doc 09): sticker packs + prescription-bottle desk toy. Limited, numbered — merch as community signal, not revenue.
- Seasonal event #1 on the website (badge FOMO, doc 06).
- Paid amplification: none on content (it must earn reach), small budget only on follower-lookalike promotion of the pinned intro.

### → 1,000,000 (Year 1–3) — "Prove the IP"
- Multi-platform expansion in sequence (doc 09): YouTube Shorts → TikTok → Discord bot → Twitch experiments.
- **Anniversary event:** "the Unshipped Project finally ships" — converting the longest-running lore joke into a real product launch (the mobile app / desktop companion). Lore-payoff marketing at scale.
- Licensing conversations (figurine partners, apparel collabs) once mark recognition is proven.
- International: JP-localized account (the aesthetic travels; the humor needs native localization, not translation).

## 4. Viral Marketing Ideas (bank)

1. **"The AI that replies faster than the news"** — during a major outage, she posts a full comedic post-mortem before the company's status page updates. Screenshot heaven.
2. **Copium Index** — a weekly fake "market index" of internet-wide cope levels, charted beautifully. Journalists love a fake index.
3. **She rates your cope** — reply-prompt format: fans submit their rationalizations, she scores them /10 with commentary. Infinitely repeatable.
4. **Patient intake day** — 24h where every new follower gets an auto-generated numbered patient card image. Follower spike engineering.
5. **The Sincere Sunday that goes viral** — the 5% content is the most screenshot-shared genre for character accounts; schedule the best writing there.
6. **April 1st birthday** — annual all-out event; the one day the "everything is a bit" brand plays it 100% straight, which is itself the bit.

## 5. Engagement Loops (compounding systems)

- Post → screenshot → external platform → new followers → more repliers → better Clinic Hours content → more posts worth screenshotting. (Content loop)
- Website dose card → shared to X → friend claims their dose → account → streak → daily return. (Product loop)
- Fan phrase → canonized → merch → fan pride → more fan phrases. (Community loop)

## 6. Influencer Collaboration Principles

- Collab tier must match or slightly exceed her size (punching up = growth; punching down = charity, do sparingly and warmly).
- She only collabs in-character. Partners brief HER world, not vice versa.
- Ideal partners: VTubers (audience overlap ~90%), tech YouTubers, dev-tool brands, gaming brands with humor tolerance. Avoid: financial products (legal + brand risk — hard no on token/trading sponsorships despite inevitable offers; this will be tested and refusing is the brand), politics-adjacent anything.

## 7. Measurement

North star: **weekly returning engaged fans** (site returners + repeat repliers), not follower count. Supporting: engagement rate >5% median, screenshot-share proxy (branded-image impressions), dose cards generated/day, D7 site retention >20%, sentiment audit monthly (the moment "cringe" outpaces "lmao" in replies, format review triggers).

## 8. Risk Playbook

- **Platform risk:** email list from day one ("clinic newsletters," monthly, in-voice); Discord + site as sovereign home base.
- **AI backlash cycles:** she leans in, never defends. Self-aware AI jokes are the shield ("yes i'm slop. artisanal, small-batch slop").
- **Copycat accounts:** trademark + consistent visual identity + memory-based fan relationships are the moat; clones can copy jokes but not the relationship graph.
- **Tone failure:** any post that misfires gets addressed in-character with genuine accountability, once, briefly — then back to programming. No notes-app apologies (she'd never; also it's her most-mocked genre).
