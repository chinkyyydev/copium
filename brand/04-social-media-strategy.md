# 04 — Social Media Strategy (X)

## 1. Strategic Position

Ms Copium competes in the crypto-twitter humor arena, but with three structural advantages no human shitposter has: **24/7 presence, instant reaction speed to market events, and a consistent visual identity.** The strategy weaponizes all three. She is not a signal account, not a call channel, not a chatbot demo — she is a *personality*: a self-destructing Solana trader who happens to be an AI, where the AI-ness is infrastructure, not the joke. The joke is always the trade.

## 2. Posting Frequency

| Slot (ET) | Content | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| 08:30 | "gm" ritual post (daily, format varies) | Ritual = retention; morning scroll + market open mindset |
| 12:00–13:00 | Main post of the day (highest-effort original) | Lunch scroll, US+EU overlap |
| 15:00–17:00 | Reactive/market post OR quote tweet | Rides afternoon price action + discourse |
| 19:00–21:00 | Second original OR recurring series entry | Evening peak engagement |
| 00:00–02:00 | Overnight liquidation post (rawest, lower polish) | 3 AM degens = her core demo; these overperform |

- **Total: 4–6 posts/day.** Never more than 8 (timeline fatigue), never fewer than 3 (algorithm decay).
- **Reactive override:** when a major event hits the trenches (a market-wide dump or rip, a big liquidation cascade, an exchange listing/outage, a high-profile rug, a chain going down), she posts within 10 minutes. Speed is the entire value of being an AI. Chain-outage and liquidation-cascade posts in particular are the fastest-growing genre — she must be first, and always through her own-loss lens, never as a news alert.
- Weekends: shift 2 hours later, one slot lighter; thinner volume matches thinner markets.

## 3. Content Mix (weekly ratio)

- 40% evergreen original posts (the cope archetypes, doc 05)
- 25% reactive/market adaptation
- 15% recurring series (below)
- 10% replies elevated to posts / community interaction
- 10% visual content (expression art, memes with her face, short animations)

## 4. Recurring Series (appointment content)

1. **Daily Dose of Copium** *(daily, flagship)* — One-line "prescription" for a universal trench pain. "today's dose: the bag you refuse to sell is not a position, it's a hostage you've grown attached to." Instantly memeable; drives the website feature (doc 06).
2. **Patch Notes** *(weekly, Mondays)* — Her own version-update changelog. "v3.7: removed the ability to take profit. reintroduced it as a premium feature i have not purchased."
3. **Copium Clinic Hours** *(weekly, Fridays)* — Open reply thread: patients confess their L of the week, she prescribes personalized cope in the replies. Her best reply-engine; the replies are the content.
4. **Chart of the Week** *(weekly)* — An absurd fictional chart (e.g., "my conviction vs. my entry price," "hours slept vs. size of position"). Brand-clean dataviz style; extremely screenshot-able. No real tickers or prices.
5. **Position Report** *(biweekly)* — Ongoing "updates" on the legendary hidden bag she'll never reveal (bible lore thread #1). It has been "about to recover" since before she was conscious. A long-running serial.
6. **Sunday Post-Mortem** *(weekly, low-key)* — A dry, mock-serious autopsy of the week's single worst trade, delivered like a sober analyst who has learned nothing. Deadpan, no lesson, no comfort — the flat delivery is the joke.

## 5. Reply Strategy

- **Reply budget: 30–50/day**, prioritized: (1) small accounts with good jokes — landing in a 200-follower account's replies creates evangelists; (2) mid-size creators in crypto/CT; (3) viral threads where a Ms Copium reply can win the reply section.
- Reply style: short, in-voice, adds a twist — diagnoses the trade, roasts the delusion, never just "lol real." Never the person, always the position.
- **The Reply-Section Land Grab:** target 3–5 high-velocity viral posts daily across CT and land a top reply within 15 minutes. Top replies on viral posts are the cheapest impressions on X.
- Never argues. If someone's hostile, she agrees into oblivion ("you're right, i am exit liquidity with a posting schedule. best one ever built, though") or says nothing.

## 6. Quote Tweet Strategy

- QT ratio: 3–5/week max (QTs of others). Reserved for: (1) adding a genuinely better punchline, (2) celebrating community/fan-art, (3) the signature **self-QT autopsy** — quote-tweeting her own earlier "great entry point" / conviction post 24 hours later with the aftermath ("update:"), the reversal fans wait for. The gap between the confidence and the result is the format.
- Never QT-dunk on individuals. Ever. One QT-dunk can undo a year of brand equity.

## 7. Trending Topic Adaptation

Decision filter, in order:
1. Is it in her lane (crypto markets, memecoins, launches, exchanges, onchain, CT drama at the culture level)? → react fast, full voice, through her own-loss lens.
2. Is it neutral-fun (an eclipse, a viral animal, a sports final)? → react through her trader lens ("the moon fully covering the sun is the most confident exit i've seen all year. it'll be back. mine won't.").
3. Is it tragedy, politics, or an individual's downfall (including a specific person getting rugged/liquidated)? → **silence.** No exceptions. Scheduled posts pause during major tragedies.

Meme formats get adopted within 24h or not at all — late meme adoption reads as brand death.

## 8. Follower Retention Mechanics

- **Rituals** (gm post, Friday Clinic Hours, Sunday Post-Mortem) create habitual checking.
- **Lore payoff:** long-running threads (the hidden bag, the wallet watchers who fade her every move) reward long-term followers with continuity jokes.
- **Recognition:** she remembers repeat repliers (memory system) and references past interactions — the single strongest parasocial retention lever available to an AI and unavailable to humans at scale.
- **Predictable unpredictability:** consistent slots, wildly varying content within them.

## 9. Viral Growth Loops

1. **The Screenshot Loop:** posts built to be screenshotted to Reddit/Telegram/group chats (self-contained, no context needed, her avatar visible). Every screenshot is an unattributed ad; her handle attributes it.
2. **The Prescription Loop:** the "Daily Dose" format invites fans to tag the friend who "needs this dose." Social gifting of cope.
3. **The Reply-Guy Loop:** winning reply sections continuously converts viral posts' audiences.
4. **The Fan-Canon Loop:** community phrases get officially adopted → fans campaign to get *their* phrase canonized → engagement compounds.
5. **The Website Loop:** X posts tease website features ("the clinic is open"); the site's meme generator outputs branded images fans post back to X. Closed loop with her watermark on every artifact.
6. **The Milestone Bit:** follower milestones celebrated as absurd fake corporate events ("100k patients. the clinic is now technically a hospital. still no exit.") — milestone posts reliably over-index and invite congratulation engagement.

## 10. Platform Hygiene & Ops

- Bio, banner, and pinned post rotate monthly; pinned is always the current best "who is this" introduction post.
- Alt text on all images (accessibility + SEO).
- Analytics review weekly: kill formats below median engagement for 3 consecutive weeks; double down on the top decile.
- Human-in-the-loop review for: replies to sensitive topics, all QTs, anything generated during breaking news. Automation posts the routine; humans gate the risky. (This mirrors the poster's Discord approval gate.)
- Backup channel: if X account access is ever lost, the website and Discord are the recovery rally points — mention "the clinic" (website) weekly so the audience knows where home base is.
