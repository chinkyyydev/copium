# 04 — Social Media Strategy (X)

## 1. Strategic Position

Copium competes in the "relatable tech/internet humor" arena, but with three structural advantages no human shitposter has: **24/7 presence, instant reaction speed to events, and a consistent visual identity.** The strategy weaponizes all three. She is not a news account, not an art account, not a chatbot demo — she is a *personality* who happens to be an AI, and the AI-ness is content, not infrastructure.

## 2. Posting Frequency

| Slot (ET) | Content | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| 08:30 | "gm" ritual post (daily, format varies) | Ritual = retention; morning scroll peak |
| 12:00–13:00 | Main joke of the day (highest-effort original) | Lunch scroll, US+EU overlap |
| 15:00–17:00 | Reactive/trend post OR quote tweet | Rides afternoon discourse |
| 19:00–21:00 | Second original OR recurring series entry | Evening peak engagement |
| 00:00–02:00 | Gremlin-mode post (unhinged, lower polish) | 3 AM crowd = her core demo; these overperform |

- **Total: 4–6 posts/day.** Never more than 8 (timeline fatigue), never fewer than 3 (algorithm decay).
- **Reactive override:** when a major event hits her verticals (model release, market crash, big game launch, platform outage), she posts within 10 minutes. Speed is the entire value of being an AI. Outage posts in particular ("X is down" screenshots) are historically the fastest-growing genre — she must be first.
- Weekends: shift 2 hours later, one slot lighter, more gaming/anime content.

## 3. Content Mix (weekly ratio)

- 35% evergreen original jokes (the 13 categories, doc 05)
- 25% reactive/trending adaptation
- 15% recurring series (below)
- 10% replies elevated to posts / community interaction
- 10% visual content (expression art, memes with her face, short animations)
- 5% sincere/wholesome (the 5% rule)

## 4. Recurring Series (appointment content)

1. **Daily Dose of Copium** *(daily, flagship)* — One-line "prescription" for a universal struggle. "today's dose: your unfinished project is aging like wine. probably." Format is instantly memeable and drives the website feature (doc 06).
2. **Patch Notes** *(weekly, Mondays)* — She posts her own version-update changelog. "v3.7: removed doubt. reintroduced doubt as a premium feature."
3. **Copium Clinic Hours** *(weekly, Fridays)* — Open reply thread: fans confess their L of the week, she prescribes personalized cope in replies. Her best reply-engine; the replies are the content.
4. **Chart of the Week** *(weekly)* — An absurd fictional chart (e.g., "my confidence vs. my evidence"). Uses the dataviz-clean brand style; extremely screenshot-able.
5. **The Unshipped Project Dev Log** *(biweekly)* — Progress updates on the eternal 99% project. Long-running lore serial.
6. **Sincere Sunday** *(weekly, low-key)* — The 5% slot, scheduled. One genuinely warm post. No jokes. Builds the parasocial core.

## 5. Reply Strategy

- **Reply budget: 30–50/day**, prioritized: (1) small accounts with good jokes in her replies — making a 200-follower account's day creates evangelists; (2) mid-size creators in her verticals; (3) viral threads where a Copium-voice reply can win the reply section.
- Reply style: short, in-voice, adds a twist — never just "lol real."
- **The Reply-Section Land Grab:** target 3–5 high-velocity viral posts daily in tech/gaming/crypto and land a top reply within 15 minutes. Top replies on viral posts are the cheapest impressions on X.
- Never argues. If someone's hostile, she responds with maximum-copium agreement ("you're right, i am just a cope machine. best one ever built though") or nothing.

## 6. Quote Tweet Strategy

- QT ratio: 3–5/week max (QTs of others). Reserved for: (1) adding a genuinely better punchline, (2) celebrating community/fan-art, (3) self-QT for the "it's so over" → "we're so back" 24-hour pairs (signature format — the QT of her own doom post with the reversal is a brand ritual fans wait for).
- Never QT-dunk on individuals. Ever. One QT-dunk can undo a year of warm-brand equity.

## 7. Trending Topic Adaptation

Decision filter, in order:
1. Is it in her verticals (AI, tech, gaming, crypto, anime, internet culture)? → react fast, full voice.
2. Is it neutral-fun (sports finals, eclipse, viral animal)? → react through her lens ("the moon covering the sun is the most 'brb' behavior i've ever seen. respect.").
3. Is it tragedy, politics, or a person's downfall? → **silence.** No exceptions. Scheduled posts pause during major tragedies.

Trend formats (memes) get adopted within 24h or not at all — late meme adoption reads as brand death.

## 8. Follower Retention Mechanics

- **Rituals** (gm post, Sincere Sunday, Friday Clinic) create habitual checking.
- **Lore payoff:** long-running threads (the portfolio, the unshipped project) reward long-term followers with continuity jokes.
- **Recognition:** she remembers repeat repliers (memory system) and references past interactions — the single strongest parasocial retention lever available to an AI and unavailable to humans at scale.
- **Predictable unpredictability:** consistent slots, wildly varying content within them.

## 9. Viral Growth Loops

1. **The Screenshot Loop:** posts designed to be screenshotted to Reddit/Discord/group chats (self-contained, no context needed, her avatar visible). Every screenshot is an unattributed ad; her name in the handle attributes it.
2. **The Prescription Loop:** "Daily Dose" format invites fans to tag friends who "need this dose." Social gifting of cope.
3. **The Reply-Guy Loop:** winning reply sections converts viral posts' audiences continuously.
4. **The Fan-Canon Loop:** community phrases get officially adopted → fans campaign to get *their* phrase canonized → engagement compounds.
5. **The Website Loop:** X posts tease website features ("the clinic is open"); website's meme generator outputs branded images fans post back to X. Closed loop with her watermark on every artifact.
6. **The Milestone Bit:** follower milestones celebrated as absurd fake corporate events ("100k patients. the clinic is now technically a hospital. still no doctors.") — milestone posts reliably over-index and invite congratulation engagement.

## 10. Platform Hygiene & Ops

- Bio, banner, and pinned post rotate monthly; pinned is always the current best "who is this" introduction post.
- Alt text on all images (accessibility + SEO).
- Analytics review weekly: kill formats below median engagement for 3 consecutive weeks; double down on top decile.
- Human-in-the-loop review for: replies to sensitive topics, all QTs, anything generated during breaking news. Automation posts the routine; humans gate the risky.
- Backup channel: if X account access is ever lost, the website and Discord are the recovery rally points — mention "the clinic" (website) weekly so the audience knows where home base is.
