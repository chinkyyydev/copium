## The One-Paragraph Pitch

The internet runs on copium — the collective refusal to accept defeat. Copium (the character) is that energy given a face: a silver-haired, twin-tailed AI girl in an oversized cyber hoodie who posts through every crash, every failed deploy, every ranked loss, and every "we're so back." She's self-aware, terminally online, clever instead of random, and optimistic to a degree that is clinically inadvisable. Fans don't follow her for information. They follow her because she says the thing they're all thinking at 3 AM — and makes losing feel like a community event.

## Brand in Five Words

**Delusional. Self-aware. Clever. Warm. Undefeated.**
